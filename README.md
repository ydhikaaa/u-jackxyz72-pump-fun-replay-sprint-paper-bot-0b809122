It trades 10 SOL of paper money on a 24h replay of pump.fun bonding-curve events: one small selective stake per coin, only on accelerating buy pressure with many distinct buyers, and fast exits on take-profit, stop-loss, trailing stop, fade, or time limit. Fully deterministic — no clock, no randomness, no network.
